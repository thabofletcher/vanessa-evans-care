How I Can Help

Care that surrounds the whole family

💜

Dementia & Alzheimer's Care

Years of long-term care experience with Alzheimer's and dementia — calm routines, gentle redirection, and care that meets your loved one wherever this day finds them.

🕊️

Comfort & Personal Care

Bathing, grooming, repositioning, feeding assistance, and the small, constant acts of tenderness that keep your loved one comfortable and dignified.

🐾

Home & Pet Care

Light housekeeping, home-cooked meals, grocery runs — and loving care for the pets who are family too, from daily walks to vet visits.

🤝

Respite for Family Caregivers

Full-time caregiving is a marathon. I step in so you can sleep, work, or simply be a daughter, son, or spouse again for a few hours.

🌙

Overnight & Vigil Support

A calm, experienced presence through the night and during the final days — so no one faces the hardest hours alone, and family can rest.

🧭

Hospice Team Coordination

When hospice begins, I work alongside the agency's nurses, social workers, and chaplains — observing, reporting changes, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Getting Started

Three gentle steps

Reach out

Call or send a message. We'll talk about your loved one, your family, and what kind of support would help most.

Meet at home

A free, no-pressure home visit to meet your family, understand the care environment, and build a plan together.

Care begins

From a few hours a week to overnight vigils, care is shaped around your family — and adjusts as needs change.
