Let's talk about your family

Consultations are free, at your home or by phone, and there's never any pressure. If I'm not the right fit, I'll help you find someone who is.

Call or text

(619) 407-0301

The fastest way to reach me is by phone. Call or text any time between 8am and 8pm — and if you're already a client family, overnight calls are always answered.

💬

What to expect when you call

We'll talk about your loved one, what your days look like right now, and where support would help most. Ten or fifteen minutes is usually enough to know whether we're a good fit.

🏡

Then a free home visit

If it feels right, I'll come meet your family, see the home, and we'll build a care plan together. No cost, and no obligation to book anything afterward.

🕐

Response time

Texts and calls are usually returned the same day. If your need is urgent, please call rather than text — I'd rather hear your voice.

A note on privacy

Please keep medical record details out of text messages. We can talk through everything by phone or in person, where your family's information stays private.
