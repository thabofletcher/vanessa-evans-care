In-home care in Tuolumne County

Tuolumne County is home. I know these roads in every season, which is not a small thing when the person you are coming to see cannot be left waiting.

The county runs from the valley floor up into the high Sierra, and care looks different at each elevation. A single-storey house in Sonora and a cabin up a private road in Twain Harte are different propositions in January, and the plan should account for that rather than discover it.

Most of my work is here, which means shorter drives, more flexible scheduling, and the ability to add a visit at short notice when something changes. For families weighing whether home care is realistic, proximity is usually the deciding factor.

Worth knowing about care in Tuolumne County

Being based in the county means same-week consultations are usually possible, and short visits are practical in a way they are not an hour away.

Care most asked for in Tuolumne County

Dementia & Alzheimer's Care

Memory loss is easier to live with in a familiar room, with a familiar face, on a familiar schedule. Most of my eleven years has been spent with people living with Alzheimer's and other dementias, and with the families trying to hold everything together around them.

End-of-Life Care

When someone is dying at home, the family should get to be the family — holding a hand, telling the stories, sitting quietly — rather than spending the last weeks managing logistics. That is the part I take on.

Long-Term Care

Most of the people I care for are not at the end of anything. They are managing a long, slow set of changes, and they would very much like to do that in their own house.

Respite Care

Most family caregivers I meet have not had a genuinely free afternoon in months. Respite is not a luxury and it is not a failure. It is the maintenance that lets you keep doing this.

Towns I serve in Tuolumne County

Sonora

As my home base, Sonora gets the most scheduling flexibility — including same-week consultations and short-notice cover.

Jamestown

Close enough to Sonora that short, frequent visits are practical — useful when someone needs a little help often rather than a lot occasionally.

Twain Harte

Mountain care needs a winter plan agreed in advance — access, firewood, walkways, and what happens when a storm closes the road.

Columbia

Quiet surroundings and short familiar routines make Columbia well suited to dementia care at home.

Groveland

Longer scheduled visits work far better than short ones at this distance — more gets done, and the arrangement is more reliable.
