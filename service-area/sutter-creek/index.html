In-home care in Sutter Creek

Sutter Creek is small, historic and full of beautiful old houses that were not built for anyone who has become unsteady.

Narrow stairs, steps between rooms, and bathrooms with no space for someone to help — these are the practical realities of caring for people in this town, and they come up in almost every first visit. The useful conversation is rarely whether to move, but what would have to change for staying to keep working.

The town being compact and walkable is a real asset. A short regular walk somewhere familiar does more for mood, appetite and steadiness than most things on a care plan, and here it is usually achievable right up until it is not.

As with the rest of Amador, longer visits and overnights suit the distance better than short daily calls, and they let enough get done to be genuinely worth it.

Care most asked for in Sutter Creek

Personal Care

The hands-on work is what a CNA is trained for, and it is the part families most often find hardest to do for a parent — not because they do not love them, but because of what it costs both people.

Dementia & Alzheimer's Care

Memory loss is easier to live with in a familiar room, with a familiar face, on a familiar schedule. Most of my eleven years has been spent with people living with Alzheimer's and other dementias, and with the families trying to hold everything together around them.

Overnight & Vigil Care

Nights are when it gets hard. Confusion is worse after dark, falls happen on the way to the bathroom, and the family member listening for every sound has not slept properly in weeks.

Respite Care

Most family caregivers I meet have not had a genuinely free afternoon in months. Respite is not a luxury and it is not a failure. It is the maintenance that lets you keep doing this.
