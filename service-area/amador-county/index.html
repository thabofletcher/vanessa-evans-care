In-home care in Amador County

Amador is the northern end of my service area, and it is where multi-hour and overnight bookings make the most sense.

Because it is the furthest reach of the area I cover, Amador works best for substantial blocks of time — a full day, a run of overnights, vigil care at the end of life — rather than an hour here and there. That is usually what families in the hardest stretches need anyway.

The towns here are small and close together, and a great many of the houses are old: narrow stairs, small bathrooms, doorways never designed for a walker. A realistic conversation about whether a house still works for the person in it is often the most useful thing I bring to a first visit.

Worth knowing about care in Amador County

Care most asked for in Amador County

Overnight & Vigil Care

Nights are when it gets hard. Confusion is worse after dark, falls happen on the way to the bathroom, and the family member listening for every sound has not slept properly in weeks.

End-of-Life Care

When someone is dying at home, the family should get to be the family — holding a hand, telling the stories, sitting quietly — rather than spending the last weeks managing logistics. That is the part I take on.

Dementia & Alzheimer's Care

Memory loss is easier to live with in a familiar room, with a familiar face, on a familiar schedule. Most of my eleven years has been spent with people living with Alzheimer's and other dementias, and with the families trying to hold everything together around them.

Respite Care

Most family caregivers I meet have not had a genuinely free afternoon in months. Respite is not a luxury and it is not a failure. It is the maintenance that lets you keep doing this.
